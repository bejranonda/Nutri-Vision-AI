## Revenue Model: Freemium → Subscription

### Subscription Tiers

| Tier | Price (Monthly) | Price (Annual) | Annual Savings |
|------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Free** | ฿0 | ฿0 | — |
| **Premium** | ฿199 | ฿1,699 (฿142/mo) | 29% |

### Enterprise / Corporate Wellness
- Custom pricing for organizations
- Employee health programs
- Group accounts with admin dashboard
- API access for health tech integrations

## Promotion / Voucher Code System

### Code Types
1. **TRIAL** — Grant time-limited access to premium features (e.g., 7-day trial)
2. **DISCOUNT** — Percentage off subscription price (e.g., 50% first month)
3. **FANCLUB** — Special codes for Shinny fanclub members and community events
4. **REFERRAL** — User-generated codes for friend invitations

### Built-in Launch Codes
| Code | Type | Benefit | Target |
|------|------|---------|--------|
| `SHINNY2024` | FANCLUB | 30-day Premium | Shinny Fanclub members |
| `EATWELL` | TRIAL | 7-day Premium trial | General marketing |
| `LAUNCH50` | DISCOUNT | 50% off first month | Launch promotion |
| `FAMILY2024` | TRIAL | 14-day Family trial | Family plan promotion |

## Revenue Streams

### B2C (Primary — Year 1 Focus)
- Free-to-paid conversion via feature gating
- Premium scans and AI coaching
- Family meal planning and shared accounts

### B2B (Year 2+)
- Corporate wellness packages
- Restaurant menu optimization consulting
- Health insurance partnerships (lower premiums for healthy eating)

### Partnership Revenue
- Delivery app integration (GrabFood, LINE MAN, foodpanda)
- Content licensing to health platforms
- Affiliate programs with healthy food brands
- Co-branded promotions with fitness apps

## Business Scalability

### Key Principle: Minimal Staff Required
- AI-powered food analysis (no manual intervention)
- Self-service promo codes (generate via admin panel)
- Automated subscription management
- Community-driven content (user recipes, tips)

### Growth Metrics to Track
- Monthly Active Users (MAU)
- Free → Premium conversion rate
- Promo code redemption rate
- Churn rate by tier
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
