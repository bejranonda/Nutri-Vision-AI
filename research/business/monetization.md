# Nutri-Vision-AI: How We Make Money

## 1. Freemium (The "Gateway" Experience)
- **Free User**: Access to basic food scanning, limited meals/scans per day, core sequencing advice, and basic health score.
- **Premium Subscription**: Unlimited scans, deeper blood sugar impact analysis, personalized recipes from **Shinny**, detailed historical tracking, and no ads.

## 2. Longevity Programs (Premium Tiers)
- **Personalized Anti-Aging Plan**: Specific sequencing and meal recommendations tailored to the user's metabolic profile (age, gender, weight, activity level).
- **Expert Coaching (AI+)**: Direct access to more advanced AI coaching based on the "Live Long to Eat Well" movement.
- **Family Plans**: Multi-user accounts with shared health dashboards and meal planning tools.

## 3. Affiliate Partnerships (Healthy Ecosystem)
- **Healthy Food/Ingredients**: Affiliates with providers of high-fiber vegetables, lean proteins, and low-GI rice (e.g., Riceberry).
- **Probiotic Providers**: Partnerships with companies offering high-quality Natto, Kimchi, or Kefir.
- **Wearables Integration**: Affiliate links for Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGM) or smartwatches that help track the "Glucose Walk" (e.g., Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin).

## 4. Corporate Wellness (B2B Offering)
- **Employee Health Dashboards**: Providing organizations with aggregated (anonymized) health data on their workforce.
- **Healthy Eating Challenges**: In-app company-wide challenges to encourage better eating habits.
- **Reduced Health Costs**: Positioning the app as a preventive tool to reduce chronic disease and improve productivity.

## 5. Exclusive Content & Masterclasses
- **Shinny's Nutrition Masterclasses**: Access to premium video content and e-books on the "Delicious in Order" philosophy.
- **Recipe Packs**: Themed recipe packs (e.g., "Thai Street Food Made Safe", "Healthy Desserts that Don't Spike Sugar").

## 6. Strategic Partnerships with Restaurants
- **EatInOrder Certified Meals**: Working with restaurants to label their menu items with the optimal eating sequence.
- **Scan-to-Order**: Integrating the app with restaurant menus for immediate sequencing advice before ordering.
